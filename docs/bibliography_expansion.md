# Bibliography Expansion Candidates

This file collects candidate references for strengthening the camera-ready
discussion. It is a support file for manuscript editing; inclusion should depend
on what the final text actually claims.

## Causal Inference Foundations

- Pearl, J. (2009). *Causality: Models, Reasoning, and Inference*.
- Hernan, M. A., & Robins, J. M. (2020). *Causal Inference: What If*.
- Peters, J., Buehlmann, P., & Meinshausen, N. (2016). Causal inference using invariant prediction.
- Schoelkopf, B., Locatello, F., Bauer, S., Ke, N. R., Kalchbrenner, N., Goyal, A., & Bengio, Y. (2021). Toward causal representation learning.

## Clinical AI Simulation And Decision Benchmarks

- Oberst, M., & Sontag, D. (2019). Counterfactual off-policy evaluation with Gumbel-max structural causal models.
- Komorowski, M., Celi, L. A., Badawi, O., Gordon, A. C., & Faisal, A. A. (2018). The Artificial Intelligence Clinician learns optimal treatment strategies for sepsis in intensive care.
- Johnson, A. E. W., Pollard, T. J., Shen, L., et al. (2016). MIMIC-III, a freely accessible critical care database.
- Johnson, A. E. W., Bulgarelli, L., Shen, L., et al. (2023). MIMIC-IV, a freely accessible electronic health record dataset.
- Wang, S., McDermott, M. B. A., Chauhan, G., et al. (2020). MIMIC-Extract: A data extraction, preprocessing, and representation pipeline for MIMIC-III.

## Fairness And Auditing Benchmarks

- Bellamy, R. K. E., Dey, K., Hind, M., et al. (2019). AI Fairness 360: An extensible toolkit for detecting and mitigating algorithmic bias.
- Bird, S., Dudik, M., Edgar, R., et al. (2020). Fairlearn: A toolkit for assessing and improving fairness in AI.
- Hardt, M., Price, E., & Srebro, N. (2016). Equality of opportunity in supervised learning.
- Chouldechova, A. (2017). Fair prediction with disparate impact: A study of bias in recidivism prediction instruments.
- Kleinberg, J., Mullainathan, S., & Raghavan, M. (2017). Inherent trade-offs in the fair determination of risk scores.

## Distribution Shift And Robustness

- Quinonero-Candela, J., Sugiyama, M., Schwaighofer, A., & Lawrence, N. D. (2009). *Dataset Shift in Machine Learning*.
- Sugiyama, M., & Kawanabe, M. (2012). *Machine Learning in Non-Stationary Environments*.
- Arjovsky, M., Bottou, L., Gulrajani, I., & Lopez-Paz, D. (2019). Invariant risk minimization.
- Gulrajani, I., & Lopez-Paz, D. (2021). In search of lost domain generalization.
